RadReady

Nuclear emergency preparedness for iPhone.

RadReady gives you the official guidance for before, during and after a nuclear incident, offline and free, with official alerts and your nearest radiation readings and shelters on a live map. RadReady Pro adds the whole monitoring network, nuclear sites, earthquakes, natural events, space weather, radon and more.
