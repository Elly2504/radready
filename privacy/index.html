Privacy Policy

RadReady · Last updated 30 August 2026

The short version: RadReady has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. RadReady does not operate a backend, does not create an account for you, and does not transmit your personal information to us. We have no ability to identify you.

Your location

If you grant location access, RadReady uses your approximate position to find nearby shelters, the closest radiation monitoring stations, and weather alerts for your area. Your coordinates are used on your device and are included in requests to the public data sources listed below so those services can return results for your area. They are not stored by us, because there is no "us" to store them — the app talks to those services directly from your phone.

You can decline location access and still use every part of the app, including the complete survival guide. You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings.

Data sources RadReady contacts

When you use the app it makes requests to these public services. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) — active alerts
  • FEMA — public shelter locations
  • US EPA RadNet — radiation monitoring data
  • Safecast — community radiation measurements
  • Radmon.org — community radiation measurements
  • Open-Meteo — wind data for fallout modelling

What is stored on your device

Your family plan, meeting points, supply checklist, saved blast scenarios, cached shelters and alerts, and your app preferences are stored locally on your iPhone. Some of this is shared with the RadReady home screen widget through a private app group container. None of it leaves your device, and all of it is removed when you delete the app.

Subscriptions

RadReady Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment and tells the app whether a subscription is active. We never see your payment details, and we receive no personal information from Apple. Apple's handling of that transaction is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Tracking

RadReady does not track you. It contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash reporting service and no third-party trackers. It does not use the Advertising Identifier and does not participate in any cross-app tracking.

Children

RadReady is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the App Store listing's support link.
