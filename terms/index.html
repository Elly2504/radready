What RadReady is

RadReady presents publicly available preparedness guidance and public data, and models the effects of a hypothetical nuclear detonation using unclassified, published approximations. It is intended for education and preparedness planning.

No warranty on the information

The guidance, radiation readings, shelter locations, weather alerts and blast calculations in RadReady are provided "as is" and without warranty of any kind. Specifically:

  • Data comes from third-party public sources that may be delayed, incomplete, offline or wrong. Radiation monitoring stations sometimes report nothing for months.
  • Blast modelling uses simplified published approximations, and the fallout plume is a rough sketch that predicts no dose. Real outcomes depend on weapon design, height of burst, terrain, weather and structures, and will differ.
  • Shelter listings are drawn from public datasets and are not verified. A listed building may be closed, unavailable, or unsuitable.
  • Potassium iodide information reproduces published FDA and CDC guidance. It is not medical advice. Take potassium iodide only when told to by officials or a healthcare provider, and consult a doctor or pharmacist about your circumstances.
  • Readings from a connected radiation detector come from that instrument and the app's conversion of them, and are indicative only. RadReady is not a medical device.
